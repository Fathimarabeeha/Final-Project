Topic : - An Intelligent Price Comparison Mobile Application 

Abstract 

The rapid growth of e-commerce platforms has made online shopping convenient, but it has also increased the difficulty of selecting the best product at the rightprice due to
variations in prices, offers, delivery options, and customer reviews across multiple websites.  Existing approaches require users to manually compare products or rely on basic 
comparison tools that provide only real-time price information without advanced analytical support. The absence of historical price analysis, future price prediction,
sentiment-based review summarization, and personalization makes the decision-making process time-consuming and inefficient. Therefore, there is a strong need for an intelligent 
and personalized system that integrates product information from multiple e-commerce platforms and provides real-time comparison along with analytical insights such as price
trends, price prediction, and sentiment analysis, enabling users to make smarter and data-driven purchasing decisions.
